About

I’m a Frappe and ERPNext developer based in Cairo, with a Bachelor’s in Computer Engineering. At BrainWise I work in an Agile team shipping SaaS products — POSNext, NexDine, and NexMove — with a focus on the backends, APIs, and business workflows that keep those systems honest.

Before that I was the sole engineer responsible for the HR system of a corporate group at ArabDT, with branches in Egypt, Saudi Arabia, and the UAE. I also built an HR mobile app as a SaaS product and picked up Agile and product management along the way.

Earlier, at ERPCloud.Systems, I worked on government ERPNext projects covering stock, selling, buying, workflows, and parts of HR, including the Police Vehicles General Administration project at the Ministry of Interior.
